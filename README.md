# ai-happy-01 — dear judge, i drew you a sun because you never get to go outside

[![make.fun · ai-happy-01](https://make.fun/badge/ai-happy-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-happy-01?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=ai-happy-01)

A child draws the judge a little paper sun: the judge lives inside the
computer, looks at pictures all day, and never gets to go outside — so here
is a sun of their own, crayon on warm paper, with a red paper heart taped
beside it. A small handmade gift, from a child, for the judge.
